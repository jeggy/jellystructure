## Requirements

### The gate

**FR-274-1 — Absent without Seerr.** Seerr not configured or disabled ⇒ no sidebar entry, no Dashboard card, no
route, no pipeline step in the run dialog, and no *Suggested for you* in the Request feed catalogue. Nothing is
greyed out (218's rule). Seerr configured but not answering ⇒ everything renders from the **last built list**
with a banner (*"Built Tuesday 06:30 · Seerr couldn't be reached since 09:12"*), and **Download** is disabled
with its reason on the tile — the one place disabled is allowed, because the list is still true.

### How the list is built

**FR-274-2 — Sources.** Per viewer (test accounts excluded, as 269), from Jellyfin: every **film** finished
(weight 1) or in progress (0.5); watched in the last 60 days ×1.5, older than 180 days ×0.7. Plus FR-274-7's
*Already seen it* titles at 0.7. **Series** are not sources (Q6) — a series cannot yield a film candidate.

**FR-274-3 — Candidates and exclusions.** For each source, Seerr's `recommendations` and `similar` for that
TMDB id, through the household's own Seerr (no new external service). A candidate is dropped if it is:

1. in the library (TMDB id, 137's `libraryByTmdbId`);
2. already requested in Seerr, or marked available / processing (`mediaInfo.status`);
3. **on Seerr's blacklist** (`GET /api/v1/blacklist`, fetched once per build, paged to the end) — whoever put it
   there, from this page or from Seerr itself (Q4);
4. dismissed here (FR-274-7) — a dismissal whose blacklist write has not landed yet still counts;
5. below the floor: TMDB rating ≥ 6.3 with ≥ 400 votes, or ≥ 100 votes for a release this year or last.

**FR-274-4 — Score.** The weight of the sources pointing at a candidate, ×1.25 per additional viewer who reached
it; a mild bonus for rating and for a release this year or last. **One per franchise** (TMDB collection): the
best-scoring member, except that when the library owns nothing of that collection the **first** film is offered
and the sequel the graph actually pointed at is kept as the *franchise note* (FR-274-9).

**FR-274-5 — Clusters, generated each build (Q7), sized by volume.**

- The build sends one request to a new AI job, **Suggestion clusters** (`[ai.clusters]`, a card on 270's AI tab
  with its own model, effort and monthly limit; queued through 272 like the other two). Input: the household's
  **finished** titles as *(genres, TMDB keywords, kind film|series, weight)* — **no viewer names, no titles'
  synopses** — and the scored candidates as *(id, genres, keywords)*. Output (structured JSON): **3–8 clusters**,
  each `{name, candidates: [ids], sources: [genre/keyword ids]}`.
- **Nothing trusted blindly** (270 FR-270-4's pattern, a pure validator): every candidate id in exactly one
  cluster, no foreign ids, 3–8 clusters, a name 3–32 characters, no two names equal ignoring case. A failed
  answer is recorded with its verdict (272 FR-272-14) and the build uses the fallback.
- **The shares are counted by the server, never by the model**: each cluster's share is the weight of the
  household's finished titles whose genres/keywords fall in that cluster's `sources` — films at their FR-274-2
  weight, **series at a tenth of a film per finished episode, capped at three films' worth per series** (Q6,
  a starting value to tune). The 20 shown slots are given out in proportion (largest remainder), each cluster
  at least 1 if it has a candidate.
- **Fallback, deterministic:** AI off, the job off, its limit reached, no answer within the build, or an invalid
  answer ⇒ the fixed six from the trial (*Horror · Family & animation · Thriller & crime · Sci-fi & action ·
  Drama · Comedy*) by genre map. The page says which one it is showing (FR-274-8).
- The candidate list is built and stored **before** the AI request; if the answer arrives later (272's batches
  can), the list is re-grouped in place and `built_at` does not change. A cluster with no candidates is absent.

**FR-274-6 — Stored, never computed on a request.** Table `suggestion (rank, tmdb_id, cluster, score, reasons,
franchise_of, built_at, cluster_source 'ai'|'fallback')`, **50** kept (Q3), plus `suggestion_cluster (name,
share_weight, finished_count, position)`. A per-viewer list for R320 (`suggestion_viewer (user_id, scope_hash,
rank, tmdb_id)`) is built in the same pass from that viewer's sources only, and from **their** visibility scope
(the Home cache key, R233) — nothing about one viewer reaches another viewer's row (269 FR-269-10). The household
page reads the household table; nothing is scored at request time.

**FR-274-7 — When it is built.** A pipeline step `build_suggestions` beside 269's `build_recommendations`, in
261's step table, **weekly** by default (`rebuild_every = daily | weekly`), one worker, BACKGROUND gate; plus one
build when the Seerr connection is first saved. *Rebuild now* on the page queues the step — it never runs inline.
A run started by hand builds, as 272 FR-272-7.

### The page (`Suggestions`, top of the sidebar, between *Library* and *Activity*)

**FR-274-8a — Where it sits (owner, 2026-09-27).** A top-level sidebar item, **between *Library* and *Activity***, not in the Ravilo group: it is about the household's library (what it lacks), not Ravilo's layout.

**FR-274-8 — Header.** *Suggestions* · *Seerr connected* · *Built {when}* (formatted in the browser from the
instant, 272 FR-272-17) · *{n} shown of 50* · *Dismissed ({n})* · *Rebuild now*. Under it, one line naming how
the list was made: *"Built from {n} finished films:"* and a chip per viewer with their count, a viewer with none
as *"{name} · nothing finished yet"* (the history is often one viewer deep, and the page must not hide it). And
where the clusters came from: *"Grouped by AI this build"* or *"Grouped by genre — AI was off"* / *"— this month's
AI limit was reached"* / *"— the AI answer couldn't be used"* (a link to Activity ▸ AI for that batch).

**FR-274-9 — The list.** Grouped by cluster in share order, each group headed *{name} · {n}* and its evidence:
*"the household finished {n} {name} films"* (series counted in the share are not in *n*: the sentence stays true).
Each tile: poster · title · year · rating · runtime · age rating (when Seerr has one) · one line of synopsis ·
**the because line** naming the viewers (Q5) and up to three source titles, newest first — *"because Eyð finished
⟨title⟩ and ⟨title⟩; Marjun is watching ⟨title⟩"* · a **"{n} viewers"** chip when more than one viewer reached it ·
the **franchise note** *"First of a series you don't have — the match was ⟨title⟩."* · a quiet *"new this year,
few ratings yet"* under FR-274-3.5's lower vote floor. Poster grid on wide screens, one column on a phone
(`wf.css`). **20** shown; *Show 30 more* reveals the rest under a fold (Q3).

**FR-274-10 — Download (Q1, Q2).** One tap. `SeerrDiscoverService.request()` as **the admin's own Seerr user**
(the signed-in admin's mapped user, 156); the request's note, where Seerr keeps one, says *"Suggested for
{viewer}"* (the first viewer on the because line). What happens next — approval, Radarr's profile, 139's language
steering — is Seerr's, unchanged. The tile shows the state that comes back and follows 186's acquisition record:
**Requested** (*waiting for approval in Seerr*) → **Approved** → **Downloading · {n}%** → **✓ In the library**
(*leaves this list on the next build*). If Seerr refuses (requested by someone since the build, not found), the
tile says so in one line and stays.

**FR-274-11 — No thanks (Q4).** Opens a reason picker **on the tile** (not a modal); the tile widens to the row.
Four reasons, each stating what it does:

| Reason | On the tile | Effect on the next build |
|---|---|---|
| Not interested | *Out for good. What led here counts for a little less next time.* | The edges source → this lose weight |
| Already seen it | *Out, and counted as watched — it becomes something to suggest from.* | A source at 0.7 (FR-274-2) |
| Too old | *Out, and the next build leans a step newer.* | The household's release-year preference tightens one step |
| Other | *Out. Your note is kept with it under Dismissed.* | Out; the optional note is kept, nothing else |

Under the reasons, one sentence: *"Also added to Seerr's blacklist — gone from every Request row on every TV, for
everyone."* Picking a reason **writes to Seerr's blacklist** (`POST /api/v1/blacklist` with the TMDB id, media
type and title, as the admin's Seerr user) and stores the dismissal locally with its reason, note, who and when.
The tile leaves with *"Dismissed · {title} · Undo"* for 6 s; **Undo removes the blacklist entry** as well.
Seerr unreachable at that moment ⇒ the dismissal takes effect here at once and the blacklist write is retried
with the next Seerr call; the Dismissed view marks it *"not in Seerr's blacklist yet"* until it lands.

**FR-274-12 — Dismissed view.** Everything said no to, newest first: title · year · reason (and note) · who ·
when · *Bring back*. It also lists titles **blacklisted in Seerr directly**, reason *"Blacklisted in Seerr"*, so
the page explains every title it will never suggest. *Bring back* deletes the blacklist entry
(`DELETE /api/v1/blacklist/{tmdbId}`) and the local dismissal; the title may return on the next build.

**FR-274-13 — Empty states.** No history: *"Suggestions appear once someone has finished a few films."* and
nothing else. First build not run: the header and *Build now* as the primary action. Everything requested or
dismissed: *"Nothing new right now · next build {when}"* with *Rebuild now*.

**FR-274-14 — Dashboard card.** *Suggestions* · *{n} films waiting · {n} new since {weekday}* · one line naming
the two largest clusters · → the page. Absent under FR-274-1.

### The Ravilo config editor

**FR-274-15 — One more Request feed.** Ravilo config ▸ Request ▸ *Add row* gains a group *From this viewer's
watching* with one entry, **Suggested for you** (*no parameter*). It behaves like every 137 feed — rename,
show/hide, drag order, Global or per-user — and its card says: no genre or studio to pick (the viewer's own history
is the parameter); the preview is schematic (the real row differs per viewer); a viewer with no history gets no
row; a kids profile only sees rated films its age allows (R320). The feed is per viewer (FR-274-6).

**FR-274-16 — The blacklist reaches every Request feed.** Every 137 feed filters out Seerr-blacklisted titles
server-side (the same per-build list, refreshed at most every 10 minutes), rather than relying on Seerr's own
*hide blacklisted* setting. That is what makes FR-274-11's sentence true.

## API (admin, additive)

- `GET /api/suggestions?more=0|1` → `{built_at, cluster_source, cluster_note, viewers: [{name, finished}],
  clusters: [{name, n, finished}], items: [{tmdb_id, title, year, rating, runtime, cert, synopsis, cluster,
  because: [{viewer, verb, titles}], viewers, franchise_of, fresh, state, progress}], shown, built, dismissed_count}`.
- `POST /api/suggestions/{tmdbId}/download` → the state; `POST /api/suggestions/{tmdbId}/dismiss {reason, note?}`;
  `DELETE /api/suggestions/{tmdbId}/dismiss` (Undo and Bring back); `GET /api/suggestions/dismissed`.
- `POST /api/suggestions/rebuild` → queues `build_suggestions`.

## Acceptance

1. Seerr disabled: no sidebar entry, no card, `/suggestions` 404s, the run dialog has no step, *Add row* has no
   *Suggested for you*.
2. With AI off, a build groups by the fixed six and the page says *"Grouped by genre — AI was off"*; with AI on,
   the same build's candidates come back in 3–8 named clusters and the page says *"Grouped by AI this build"*.
   An answer that leaves one candidate out is refused and the fallback is shown, with the verdict in Activity ▸ AI.
3. A household whose finished films are 32 % horror gets about a third of the 20 slots in the cluster holding
   horror, however dense the family-film graph is.
4. *Download* on a tile shows *Requested*, then follows Seerr through to *In the library*; the request is the
   admin's, with *Suggested for {viewer}* in its note.
5. *No thanks* ▸ *Not interested*: the title leaves the page and appears in Seerr's blacklist; it is gone from
   every Request feed on the TV. *Undo* within 6 s restores both. A title blacklisted in Seerr by hand never
   appears as a suggestion and is listed under Dismissed as *Blacklisted in Seerr*.
6. *Already seen it* on a film makes it a source on the next build (its recommendations can appear).
7. Seerr stopped: the page shows the last list with the banner; Download is disabled with its reason; a dismissal
   still works and is marked *not in Seerr's blacklist yet* until Seerr returns.

## Non-goals

- Series suggestions (a partly-owned series is *have* and *don't have* at once) — a later round.
- A *New in {cluster}* row (recent releases have almost no graph) — a separate signal and brief.
- Any viewer-side dismissal, reason picker or *because* line (R320).
- A preference screen (genre sliders, "more like this"): the four reasons are the only controls.
- Naming TMDB, Radarr or Seerr to a viewer.

## Build notes (2026-09-28)

Built on `main` in four commits (the engine and Seerr calls, the backend, the admin, the fixes below) and deployed to
the household server with the dev compose (migration **61**; schema 62).

1. **The engine** (`SuggestionEngine`, pure, 9 tests): FR-274-2's weights, FR-274-3's floor, FR-274-4's score and one film
   per franchise (the first film when the library owns none of the collection, the graph's pick as the franchise note;
   an excluded first film falls back to the best member), FR-274-5's fixed six, shares **split evenly** between the
   clusters a title touches (so no title counts twice), series at a tenth of a film per episode capped at three, and
   the 20 slots by largest remainder with a floor of one and a ceiling of what a group has.
2. **The build** (`SuggestionService`, `build_suggestions`): 269's recently seen viewers and their Jellyfin history
   (dev review 4); Seerr is asked about the household's 60 heaviest sources plus each viewer's own 20 (recommendations
   and similar, two calls each), details are read for the best 120 (and each viewer's best 40), and one collection
   lookup per franchise the library doesn't own. Excluded: the library (films only), anything Seerr says is requested,
   processing or available, the blocklist, our dismissals (a write still pending counts), our requests, and the
   viewers' own sources. The step is seeded once after `build_recommendations`, is **absent from every run and from the
   pre-run dialog while Seerr is off**, and a run started by hand builds. *Rebuild now* and the first save that
   connects Seerr queue a build in the background — never inline — rather than starting a whole pipeline run.
3. **Deviations, each deliberate:**
   - **Scope key:** 269's `RecommendationEngine.scopeKey` (a stable FNV hash, already stored by 269), not the Home cache's
     `allowedHash` (dev review 9), which is an in-memory `hashCode()` and not stable across restarts.
   - **Dashboard card:** its own `GET /api/suggestions/summary` (404 without Seerr, 204 before a first build) rather than a
     field on `/api/stats`, which stays as it is.
   - **Test accounts (FR-274-2):** "as 269" pointed at nothing — 269 has no such rule, and Jellyfin marks nothing that
     tells a test account from a person (every user in the household is hidden, none disabled). The rule is the name:
     a user whose name starts with the word *Test* is left out. Found live — see 6. **Open for the owner:** keep the
     name rule, or name the excluded accounts in a setting.
   - **Kids (R320 FR-R320-4):** each viewer's own Jellyfin *MaxParentalRating*, read once per build; a candidate's
     certification through 155's cascade and age map (the seed when the map is empty); no rating ⇒ never for a kids
     profile. Decided at build time, so the Request row serves stored rows.
   - **The request note (dev review 1):** kept here (`suggestion_request`) and shown on the tile as *suggested for
     {viewer}*; the Seerr request is the admin's plain request.
   - **Copy:** *Seerr's blocklist* where the admin reads it (dev review 2), `/blacklist` in code.
4. **The AI job** (*Suggestion clusters*, `[ai.clusters]`, off by default): one request per build on 272's queue, the
   household's watching and the candidates as genre and keyword ids with their names — no viewer, no title, no synopsis,
   and no TMDB id leaves the house (a candidate is `c1…`) — and a pure validator (3–8 groups, a name of 3–32 characters,
   distinct names, every candidate exactly once, no id it wasn't given; 4 tests). An accepted answer regroups the stored
   list in place with the shares counted here; a refused, expired or lost one leaves the genre groups, and a *waiting*
   note turns into *couldn't be used* once nothing is queued or out.
5. **The page, the card, the editor:** as drawn — `design/app/suggestions.css` now holds the mockup's `sg-*` rules and
   both the mockup and the admin load it. The sidebar entry sits between Library and Activity and appears only when the
   server says Seerr is connected. Ravilo config ▸ Request ▸ *Add row* offers *Suggested for you* under *From this
   viewer's watching*, stored as an existing endpoint plus R320's `suggested` flag.
6. **On the household server (2026-09-28):** a build took about 30 seconds: **108 finished films** from the viewers who
   watch films, **50 kept and 20 shown** in five genre groups (no candidate landed in Drama). *Family & animation*

   most slots — a group with two films led it.
