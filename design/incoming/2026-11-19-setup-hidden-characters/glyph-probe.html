Mark glyphs: what the self-hosted faces actually carry

FR-4 names U+23CE for a line break and U+00B7 for every other hidden character. The three woff2 in design/app/fonts/ are subsets. Measured in the renderer, not assumed, and the test is cross-family: three different type designs give a codepoint they really carry three different advance widths, while a codepoint none of them carries is drawn by one fallback face for all three and measures identically. The control, U+0041, is the row to read the table against.
