Your Samsung TVs — the phone's overview after approval

Three directions for the one screen the Ravilo Android phone shows when the viewer has an approved Samsung TV: which TVs are on this Wi-Fi, which one is approved, what version it is running and what the latest is. Drawn 2026-11-23. 412 × 915 dp portrait, the Android phone and no other surface.

What is fixed

  • One screen, titled Your Samsung TVs. It starts the subnet scan by itself and says Looking for TVs on this Wi-Fi… until the scan ends.
  • One card or row per TV found: friendly name, model, and a badge — Approved (green), Waiting for approval, Not approved, Not set up.
  • The approved TV shows On the TV: 1.70 and Latest: 1.71, and one primary button: Install on TV when Ravilo is not on it or the version is not known; Update to 1.71 when it is older; no button, the line Up to date and a quiet Reinstall link when they are equal. An approved TV no release covers yet shows Comes with the next Ravilo release in place of Latest and has no button.
  • A request whose TV the scan did not find is listed from the server with Not found on this Wi-Fi, no version and no button. Below the list, one quiet line with the count found and Search again.
  • An installed version the phone cannot read shows as On the TV: —.
  • One action and one big primary button per screen; under about 40 words of body text; light and dark; readable at large font sizes. No connection detail, no IP address. "Developer Mode" is the only technical term allowed, and this screen does not need it.
  • No string names Jellyfin. Danish and Faroese drafts for every new string.
  • The phone never infers a state. Badges come from the server, versions from the TV.

Before and after

The brief gained this on the day of the round, from the owner: "this doesn't provide good information. So let's make sure to keep it clean but most importantly provide useful information." The "before" is the shipped Ready screen — GuideScreen.Ready, drawn by Page(…, art = Art.TV, body = "samsungtv.ready.body", cta = "samsungtv.ready.cta"). No capture of it is in this repository and none ever will be; the one the owner sent shows his own profile picture. What follows describes our own shipped strings and our own drawing, which is all the comparison needs.

Today: a generic TV drawing of roughly 142 × 102 dp, about a third of everything drawn; the title Your TV is approved; two lines of standing advice; and the button Install on TV. It states one fact, and it is the only one it can state — the screen holds no TV identity and no version, so it cannot name the TV, cannot choose its own verb, and writes advice where state should be.

Today's screen spends about a third of its height on a drawing that carries no information, and states one fact. A's card spends none on decoration and states six — and the illustration is the only thing removed to make room. All six come from data the app already has; no new wire field.

The glyph option. The note says to drop or shrink the illustration unless it carries information, and that a small TV glyph on each card is fine. A as drawn carries none at all, which is the far side of the same line. a-x4-glyph and a-x5-glyph-update draw the picked direction with a 34 dp tile — radius 10, rgba(fg,.07), a 19 dp TV glyph in --ink-dim — at the head of each card's name row. It carries nothing the name does not; it gives the card an anchor for the eye. An option on the picked direction, not a change to it: the owner picked A from pictures with no tile.

The three directions

A — Stack Recommended

Cards. The approved TV's card is the big one — radius 18 instead of 14, more padding, and the only card carrying a button. Two labelled version lines, label left in Sora, value right in JetBrains Mono. The badge sits at the end of the name row, so the badges form one column down the right edge. Everything one TV needs is inside that TV's own card.

B — Ledger

No cards. Full-bleed rows on hairlines, every TV the same weight, the way the phone's other settings lists look. The version pair is one line, 1.70 › 1.71. The one primary button is pinned to the bottom of the screen with the TV's name above it.

C — Signal

The state is the headline and the TV is the kicker above it: Update waiting, Up to date, Ready to install, Waiting for the next release, Not on this Wi-Fi. Versions are one labelled inline pair under it. The button is inside the block. Every other TV is a compact row under Also on this Wi-Fi.

The green, and the one new token

The badge green is #2dd49a — the shipped --good — on every dark skin. On the Daylight theme that colour reaches about 1.9 : 1 on its own 16 % tint, so Daylight takes #0f7a52 at 14 %. The red for Not approved is #f2797f dark and #b3242c light. A light-theme green is a real token the phone does not have yet — the admin system already carries exactly this override on .badge, and Ravilo's RaviloColors will need one.

Decided in this round, and why

RowDecision
Card orderApproved/Ready, then Waiting for approval, then Not approved, then Not set up; the server-only rows keep that rank among themselves and sit below every found TV. Equal ranks keep the server's order so two scans do not reshuffle the list. The spec fixes only that server-only rows come last, and the first render of state 7 put a Not set up TV above the viewer's own approved one.
No Wi-FiNo card says Not found on this Wi-Fi, and the footer is Try again, not Search again. There was no scan.
LoadingTwo placeholder cards under the scan line, not a bare spinner: a 254-host sweep is a state a viewer meets, not a frame.
Up to dateUp to date and Reinstall share one 44 dp line. Stacked, 40 dp apart, they read as unrelated.
Word valuesA value that is a word, not a version — Not installed — is set in Sora. JetBrains Mono made it look like something the TV returned.
"Wi-Fi"Never breaks at its hyphen. In Compose that is a non-breaking hyphen in the string.

Open, for Bárður

  1. Render the approved card from the server while the scan runs? The request rows are already in hand — they are what decided this screen opens at all. Drawn as a-x1-serverfirst: the approved card appears at once with a short bar where On the TV will be, and one small placeholder stands for whatever else the sweep finds. It is a better first second than two grey rectangles, and it needs a sentence in FR-10 that is not there.
  2. FR-10 has no line for "Ravilo is not on this TV". FR-11 reads a 404 as not installed, which is distinct from not known, and it is the common case. Drawn as a-x2-notinstalled with the draft string Not installed. Both states give the same button, so this is copy, not behaviour.
  3. Two approved TVs. Nothing in the brief or the spec says this cannot happen — the cap is ten rows. A is the only direction that holds it without a new rule.

What this round did not draw

  • Any surface but the Android phone. The TV, the desktops, the web, the cast receiver, the Tizen app and the admin have no such screen.
  • The Midnight, Noir and Graphite skins. Drawn in Daylight and Aurora only; the round's two new colours are declared per theme and will need a value in the other three.
  • A focus state. This surface is touch-only — there is no D-pad on the phone's guide.
  • An error state of its own. Every failure on this screen belongs to FR-7's install, which already has one sentence and one button per failure, and is a different screen.
  • The iPhone 16 bezel. The Profile row is Android-only.
