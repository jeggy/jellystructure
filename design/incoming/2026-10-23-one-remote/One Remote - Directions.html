One remote, whether you cast it or open something already playing

Ravilo has two remotes. The one you get when you cast, and the one you get when you open something that is already playing somewhere else. The phone that started a cast has both at once — the cast bar opens one, that playback's row in Playing everywhere opens the other. This round draws the one remote that replaces them, on the phone, the web and the Mac.

Recommendation: direction A — the kind picks the remote. Every kind gets the Cast remote's layout; the kind decides what goes in it — the transport, the two bars a book needs, the lyrics a song has — and each gains the controls only the session remote has today. How the place is reached — a cast link or the server — is never drawn, never named, and never changes a pixel. Direction B is drawn for the film and the music frame so the cost is visible rather than asserted, and it is a real alternative, not a straw man.

Revision 3 — the owner has decided, and music comes back to the cast remote. “The current cast remote is the nice looking one and todays playing page is a bit bad UI, so that one goes. [Its] functionality needs to be there.” So today’s Playing page is retired everywhere, including for music playing on this phone, and the music frames are the cast remote’s shape with everything that page did: Playing from, the version chips, ♥, ⋯, the swipe, shuffle · previous · ⏯ · next · repeat, the lyrics, the queue and the book variant. Revision 2’s musicPlaying builder is gone from or-kit.js and a-music-today is gone from the package: a drawing of a retired page is a specification of a screen that no longer exists. The film half is untouched as a design — its pixels moved only for the footer, below. Volume is now an on-screen slider everywhere, and decision 9 draws its two outcomes.

Revision 2 drew music on the shipped Playing page. That was the wrong correction and it was Bárður’s call, not the designer’s; it is recorded here because the package no longer contains it. Round 1’s music frames are what revision 3 starts from.

What is fixed before a line is drawn

None of this is a design choice. It comes out of the constitution and the shipped specs, and every frame in this round holds it.

FixedWhat it does to these frames
Server-pushed state only — no derived or optimistic local state The remote shows what the place reported and nothing it worked out for itself. A press shows that it is in flight — the 400 ms dim, then a spinner past 1 s — and never a predicted result. This is why moving, couldn’t move and lost contact keep the ⏯ glyph they had: the last state the place reported still stands, and drawing ▶ there would be the frontend inventing a pause.
Never the word “session” Places and people are named. Stue TV, Stue + Gæsteværelse, Olivar.
Unsupported is absent; unreachable is greyed Both rules are in these pictures and they do not collide. A control the place cannot do is not drawn at all — a film has no shuffle anywhere in direction A. A transport that cannot reach the place right now is drawn and greyed, with Try again. The single exception is a room that does not report its volume: the place exists and simply cannot answer, so the slider is drawn, disabled, and the reason is a line under it.
No ink on a picture The art is a card on solid ground — 16:9 for a film or an episode, square for music or a book — and it falls back to a wordmark tile with no redraw. The payoff is that this surface light-themes, which the hero card and the film player cannot.
Leaving the remote never stops playback Stopping is only ever explicit, from the footer. On someone else’s it asks once, inline.
46 dp minimum on the phone Every target in frame: the place pill is 46, the skip buttons 56, ⏯ 72, the footer 60.
The web has no Chromecast Its places are Ravilo apps reached through the server, so Stue TV never appears in a web frame. The web frames point at Soveværelse TV and at Pixel 9.
The TV is never a remoteNot in this round at all.

Direction A — the kind picks the remote recommended

One screen, one builder, and the only thing a cast and a server place disagree about is the name in the pill. A1 and A2 are rendered from the same call with one field different, which is why one-remote-a-identity-pair.png is in the package: “it is the same screen” is a claim a pair settles and a single frame cannot.

What it is, top to bottom

  • One header control, and it names the place. Stue TV, Soveværelse TV, Stue + Gæsteværelse. Tapping it opens Move to…, which holds Play here. There is no device chip beside it, because a second control that also names the place is how you end up with two remotes again.
  • The art card, 16:9 or square by kind, on solid ground.
  • Kicker, title, state line. The state line says what it is doing — or, when the playback is someone else’s, whose it is, because that is the thing that changes what the screen means.
  • A draggable seek bar with elapsed and remaining.
  • The transport the kind needs. A film or an episode gets −10 s · ⏯ · +30 s. Music gets shuffle · prev · ⏯ · next · repeat. Neither gets the other’s.
  • Volume, inline under the transport on a film or an episode. One place, one slider. Several rooms, a master labelled Volume and a room row each, every room with its own mute. Revision 3: it is inline on every kind, music included — see below.
  • A footer of at most three, on a film or an episode: Audio & Subs · Next episode (series only) · Stop; on a song Up next · Lyrics · Stop; on a book Chapters · Add bookmark · Stop. Revision 3 makes the button a two-row grid — a fixed 20 px icon row and a label row under it — rather than reserving two lines of label. With the shipped player.audio_subs English now fits one line and the footer is 16 px shorter; Lyd & undertekster and Ljóð & undirtekstir are still about 112 and 118 px against 108 and still wrap, and with the grid their icons stay on one baseline anyway. One geometry, three languages. fo-a-nextup is the proof frame.

Music and a book: the cast remote’s shape, with everything the Playing page did revision 3

A song is the SAME builder call as a film — the header with the place pill, the art card, the title block, the seek bar, the transport, the volume block, the footer — and the kind decides what goes in each slot. What music brings in from the retired page, all of it read off MusicPlayingScreen and R322:

  • Playing from {album} with the ♪, on its own line under the header (the shipped music.from). On a book that line is the book’s title, as BookPlayingScreen draws it.
  • The version chips, the artist and the album as one line under the title — R344’s .rv-v, shipped — and ♥ level with the title, which is where Credits() puts it.
  • ⋯, the track menu, in the header’s right slot (decision 8).
  • Swipe the cover for next/previous. Drawn as the mid-gesture frame (a-music-swipe): 34 % across at the alpha SwipeCover computes, the next cover underneath. A still frame cannot show a gesture, and the shipped page has no swipe hint to draw instead.
  • Shuffle · previous · ⏯ · next · repeat, already direction A’s music transport.
  • Lyrics — the head shrinks to a 52 dp cover and the title and the synced lines take the space (FR-R322-7, 24 sp Space Grotesk; the sung line lit, what has been sung dim, what is coming secondary). The footer’s Lyrics lights. a-music-lyrics.
  • Up next, as a sheet over the remote, with drag handles and the shipped {n} songs · {t} left computed from the queue. a-music-upnext.
  • Nothing playing yet — the last-played song, loaded paused where it was left (FR-R322-3), no state line and no Stop. a-music-idle.
  • A book — the chapter as the title, the chapter’s seek bar, the whole-book scrubber (BOOK, elapsed / total), … left, speed · −30 s · ⏯ · +30 s · sleep, and the chapter strip. a-book.
  • Volume is INLINE, the same block as the film’s. Revision 2 measured that it would not fit the shipped Playing page — 108 px of slack against a 154 px block — and that measurement still holds of that page, which is why it is no longer the question: the cast remote’s composition is built around the block, and the square art shrinks to make room for it rather than the transport going below the fold. What it costs is visible: the cover is about 180 px with two rooms, 240 with one, and about 120 on a book. Open question 2.

What it costs

  • Two code paths, not one. A film remote and a music remote are two layouts that share every component. That is the trade: the controls fit the thing you are controlling, and nobody has to look at a shuffle button on a film.
  • The place is named twice on a plain playing frame — once in the pill and once in Playing on Stue TV. That is the shipped cast remote’s own shape and the shipped string, so it is kept; see the open questions.
  • The cast loses its volume rule. Today a cast has no on-screen slider on purpose. Decision 1 is exactly that question, and it is the owner’s.

Why not “fix only the doors”

Making the cast bar and the Playing everywhere row open the same remote fixes the one phone that started the cast and leaves the second remote standing on every other device. It was ruled out in the brief and nothing in the drawing argues with that.

Direction B — today’s session remote, extended

The single scrolling page becomes the one remote for everything. It gains ±skip, the next-up countdown and the full tracks sheet, and a cast opens it too. Two frames, so the cost is in a picture.

AxisB · the session remote extended A · the kind picks the remote (recommended)
A film’s transport Shuffle, previous and next are drawn for a film, because today’s page draws them for every kind. ±skip lands as a second transport row under the first. −10 s · ⏯ · +30 s, large and centred. Nothing a film cannot do is on screen.
Fits the screen No. In b-music the queue, Play here and Stop are below the fold at 412 × 915 — you scroll to stop something. Yes, in every state and in all three languages. The tallest stack — three rooms, one of them not reporting, in Faroese — fits by letting the square art shrink.
Where the place is named Twice already today: the sheet header Playing on Stue TV and the place line under the title. Twice on a plain playing frame, and the brief’s decision 2 is whether to trim it to one.
Subtitles Chips, which have to grow into the two-level sheet with flags, badges and S · M · L — at which point the chips are a door to the sheet and not a control. One footer button opening the component the local player already opens, with one extra line: Applies on {place}.
How much changes Less. It is the page that exists, with rows added. More. But the Cast remote already exists too, and A is mostly deleting the second one.
What a viewer notices Casting now looks like the thing in the sheet. Nothing, on the phone that casts. That is the point.

The one mini bar

Today there are two: a cast bar and an “elsewhere” bar. These are one component rendered twice — art, title, the place line, a +N chip when something else is playing too, and ⏯. Nothing in it says how the place is reached, and the cast glyph is gone from it. That is what stops the two bars opening two remotes.

Music’s bar is the same component in music mode, above Listen · Browse · Playing · Queue · Profile. Its second line is the place, not the artist: the shipped bar puts the artist in front of the place with .spk-on { flex: none }, and at 412 with a two-room line the place takes the whole row, collapses the artist to nothing and still clips — the bar reads · 🔊 Stue + Gæstevær…, with a stray dot where the artist was.

Web and Mac

  • Web, 1440 — a film. The same blocks in two columns — art on the left, the whole control stack on the right — against Soveværelse TV, with volume as an inline row under the transport. No Chromecast anywhere on this surface.
  • Web, 1440 — a song. The desktop Playing page, not a wide phone: MusicPlayingScreen branches on isDesktopLayout before it ever reaches the portrait page, so the web at a desk width is the cover beside the lyrics, with the capsule.
  • Web, 390. The phone layout, unchanged. Which is the point: it is not a third design.
  • Mac — a film. The Playing page is the remote: the same blocks, two columns, the capsule along the bottom.
  • Mac — a song. The shipped desktop Playing page, which is the cast remote at a desk width and is the one place the Playing page’s shape survives, because the lyrics beside the cover are the desktop’s own strength: the 380 px cover with the title, the version chips, the place pill, the seek line, the transport and — new in revision 3 — the volume block under it, with the lyrics beside it in 30 px. Stop is in the capsule, not the page — the left column is 380 px and the place pill alone fills it, and the capsule puts Stop one tap from every page, not only this one. The capsule’s transport follows the kind too: previous/next on a song, ±10/30 s on a film. Both desktop music frames are rendered taller than a real window so the whole page is visible; see open question 1.
  • The capsule’s volume popover holds the same sliders, drawn over Films, because a popover you open from the capsule is for when you are not on the Playing page; over the Playing page it put the same three sliders on screen twice.

Decisions — the lean is drawn

DecisionDrawn (the lean)Alternative
1 · Volume on a film playing on a TV One slider, as for speakers; the hardware keys drive it on the phone. In every film frame. Keep the Cast rule — no on-screen slider for a TV, hardware keys and a transient pill only. Drawn, revision 2: a-cast-film-no-slider, the same picture with the volume row removed and the pill showing Stue TV · 60 %. The argument for it is still live: on a cast, volume belongs to the TV, and a slider invites a fight with the TV’s own remote. Caveat: no such hardware-key pill was found anywhere in ravilo-ui; the frame draws the shipped toast carrying that line.
2 · Device chip vs place line One control that names the place and opens Move to… — at the top on a film, in the device chip’s own row on a song — with Stop one tap away for every kind: the footer on a film, the bottom row on a song, the capsule on the desktop. Keep both — a chip for switching or stopping and a line for moving. Not drawn: two controls naming the same place is the shape this round exists to remove.
3 · Someone else’s playback The same remote, transport absent, state line Olivar is watching, one card saying why. A smaller read-only card. The drawn frame shows what the lean costs: a lot of empty screen. That emptiness is honest — there is nothing to press — but it is the strongest argument the alternative has, and it is visible in a-someone-else.
4 · Stop’s word Stop everywhere, the shipped cast.stop_room string. Every footer in the round. Keep Stop casting on a cast — which would mean the cast frame and the elsewhere frame differ by one word, and the identity pair would stop being a pair.
5 · Where the Playing tab’s remote sits revision 3 In the tab, with the bar under it — FR-R322-2: a page, no close chevron, its slot held open and empty. Every music frame in the package except one. a-music, a-book. Full-screen, chevron back, no bar — a-music-full. It is worth looking at: the bar costs 92 px and the cover is 70 px bigger without it. It is also what the remote already is when it is opened over something else, so the product needs both drawings whichever way this goes.
6 · The bottom bar’s Queue tab revision 3 Kept. The sheet (a-music-upnext) is the list you glance at from the remote and dismiss; the tab is the one you go to deliberately, and it is where Clear queue belongs — a destructive action does not live under a list you opened to look at. Fold it in and leave only the sheet, freeing a fifth of the bar. Not drawn.
7 · The volume row on This phone revision 3 Present, one row, the phone’s own — a-music-here. The frame exists to prove local and remote are one screen, and a block that disappears when the place is the phone is the exact thing that makes them two. Absent, on the argument that the hardware keys already do it and the platform draws its own slider. Not drawn, deliberately: see the pair.
8 · ⋯, the track menu revision 3 In the header’s right slot, which was already an empty 46 dp ghost. In the footer beside the three labelled buttons — where BottomRow puts it on the shipped page. Not drawn: a fourth, bare glyph next to three labelled buttons reads as a fourth action of the same weight.
9 · The volume slider’s two outcomes revision 3 (a) Released, not confirmed yet: the thumb stays where the finger left it and NOTHING is marked. (b) The place never took it: the thumb goes back and one short line says so for 3 s — {place} kept {n} %. Four frames: a-vol-pending-film, a-vol-reverted-film, a-vol-pending-music, a-vol-reverted-music. A mark while waiting — a spinner, a ghost thumb, a greyed value. Not drawn: it would warn about the 95 % of drags that work, on a screen the viewer is holding. On the revert the line is not optional: a thumb that walks back on its own with nothing said reads as a bug rather than a report.
10 · A place that cannot report its volume revision 3 — in the value, the slider drawn and disabled, and the shipped volume.no_report as the line under it. a-film-no-report (Soveværelse TV, sound to a soundbar). Absent. Not drawn, and the rule is in the fixed table above: the place EXISTS and simply cannot answer, which is a different fact from “this cannot be done”.

Open, for Bárður

  1. New, revision 3 — the volume block does not fit the desktop Playing page at a real window height. With three rooms inline the Mac window needs about 1040 px and the browser about 960; at 900 the second and third room rows go under the capsule. Both frames are rendered tall so the whole page is visible and this is not hidden. Either the page scrolls there, which a window may legitimately do, or on the desktop volume stays in the capsule’s popover where it already ships. Not a judgement this round should make.
  2. New, revision 3 — three sliders inline cost the cover. At 412 the square cover shrinks to about 180 px with two rooms and a master, against the 330 px the shipped page gives it; the one-room frame keeps 240 and the book, which carries two bars and a chapter strip, drops to about 120. Compare a-music, a-music-here and a-book. It is the honest cost of the owner’s on-screen slider and it is worth his eye.
  3. New, revision 3 — the idle page says Playing from Harbour Lights while nothing is playing. That is the shipped code (MusicPlayingScreen falls back to music.now_playing only when there is no context at all), and the code wins, so the frame draws it. It still reads wrong. A string change, not a design one.
  4. Does the state line keep Playing on {place} when the pill above it already names the place? The round keeps it, because it is the shipped string and the shipped cast remote’s own shape. Trimming it to Playing is one line of i18n and it would remove the only repetition left in the lean.
  5. The three-up footer wraps in every language. Settled in revision 2: all three buttons are the same two-line height. Re-settled in revision 3: the footer maps onto the shipped player.audio_subs and the button becomes a two-row grid, so English gets one line and Danish and Faroese keep two with their icons still on one baseline. Nothing is truncated in any of the three.
  6. New, revision 2 — the hardware-key pill. Decision 1’s alternative draws a transient Stue TV · 60 %, and no such pill exists anywhere in ravilo-ui; the frame draws the shipped toast carrying that line. Whether it exists or has to be built is for you and Sjúrður.
  7. New, revision 2 — Stop on the desktop is in the capsule, and mac-film still carries one in the Playing page’s footer. At adoption that is one or the other; the accepted film frame was not touched.
  8. Noir on the phone is a darker background and nothing else. ravilo.css inverts the accent to amber for noir; mobile/ravilo-phone.css does not. The round reports the difference rather than inventing an amber phone — noir-a-cast-film is in the package as the evidence.
  9. Decision 1 is the one with real weight. The shipped reason for no slider on a cast — that volume belongs to the TV and a slider fights its own remote — is still true. The lean is drawn because the round was asked to draw it, not because the argument against it got weaker.
