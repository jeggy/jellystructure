Ravilo · phone, web, Mac · directions · 2026-10-23

One remote, whether you cast it or open something already playing

Ravilo has two remotes. The one you get when you cast, and the one you get when you open something that is already playing somewhere else. The phone that started a cast has both at once — the cast bar opens one, that playback's row in Playing everywhere opens the other. This round draws the one remote that replaces them, on the phone, the web and the Mac.

Recommendation: direction A — the kind picks the remote. A film or an episode gets the Cast remote's layout; a song or a book gets the music Playing page; each gains the controls only the session remote has today. How the place is reached — a cast link or the server — is never drawn, never named, and never changes a pixel. Direction B is drawn for the film and the music frame so the cost is visible rather than asserted, and it is a real alternative, not a straw man.

What is fixed before a line is drawn

None of this is a design choice. It comes out of the constitution and the shipped specs, and every frame in this round holds it.

FixedWhat it does to these frames
Server-pushed state only — no derived or optimistic local state The remote shows what the place reported and nothing it worked out for itself. A press shows that it is in flight — the 400 ms dim, then a spinner past 1 s — and never a predicted result. This is why moving, couldn’t move and lost contact keep the ⏯ glyph they had: the last state the place reported still stands, and drawing ▶ there would be the frontend inventing a pause.
Never the word “session” Places and people are named. Stue TV, Stue + Gæsteværelse, Olivar.
Unsupported is absent; unreachable is greyed Both rules are in these pictures and they do not collide. A control the place cannot do is not drawn at all — a film has no shuffle anywhere in direction A. A transport that cannot reach the place right now is drawn and greyed, with Try again. The single exception is a room that does not report its volume: the place exists and simply cannot answer, so the slider is drawn, disabled, and the reason is a line under it.
No ink on a picture The art is a card on solid ground — 16:9 for a film or an episode, square for music or a book — and it falls back to a wordmark tile with no redraw. The payoff is that this surface light-themes, which the hero card and the film player cannot.
Leaving the remote never stops playback Stopping is only ever explicit, from the footer. On someone else’s it asks once, inline.
46 dp minimum on the phone Every target in frame: the place pill is 46, the skip buttons 56, ⏯ 72, the footer 60.
The web has no Chromecast Its places are Ravilo apps reached through the server, so Stue TV never appears in a web frame. The web frames point at Soveværelse TV and at Pixel 9.
The TV is never a remoteNot in this round at all.

Direction A — the kind picks the remote recommended

One screen, one builder, and the only thing a cast and a server place disagree about is the name in the pill. A1 and A2 are rendered from the same call with one field different, which is why one-remote-a-identity-pair.png is in the package: “it is the same screen” is a claim a pair settles and a single frame cannot.

What it is, top to bottom

  • One header control, and it names the place. Stue TV, Soveværelse TV, Stue + Gæsteværelse. Tapping it opens Move to…, which holds Play here. There is no device chip beside it, because a second control that also names the place is how you end up with two remotes again.
  • The art card, 16:9 or square by kind, on solid ground.
  • Kicker, title, state line. The state line says what it is doing — or, when the playback is someone else’s, whose it is, because that is the thing that changes what the screen means.
  • A draggable seek bar with elapsed and remaining.
  • The transport the kind needs. A film or an episode gets −10 s · ⏯ · +30 s. Music gets shuffle · prev · ⏯ · next · repeat. Neither gets the other’s.
  • Volume, inline under the transport. One place, one slider. Several rooms, a master labelled Volume and a room row each, every room with its own mute.
  • A footer of at most three. Subtitles & audio · Next episode (series only) · Stop for a film; Up next · Lyrics · Stop for music.

What it costs

  • Two code paths, not one. A film remote and a music remote are two layouts that share every component. That is the trade: the controls fit the thing you are controlling, and nobody has to look at a shuffle button on a film.
  • The place is named twice on a plain playing frame — once in the pill and once in Playing on Stue TV. That is the shipped cast remote’s own shape and the shipped string, so it is kept; see the open questions.
  • The cast loses its volume rule. Today a cast has no on-screen slider on purpose. Decision 1 is exactly that question, and it is the owner’s.

Why not “fix only the doors”

Making the cast bar and the Playing everywhere row open the same remote fixes the one phone that started the cast and leaves the second remote standing on every other device. It was ruled out in the brief and nothing in the drawing argues with that.

Direction B — today’s session remote, extended

The single scrolling page becomes the one remote for everything. It gains ±skip, the next-up countdown and the full tracks sheet, and a cast opens it too. Two frames, so the cost is in a picture.

AxisB · the session remote extended A · the kind picks the remote (recommended)
A film’s transport Shuffle, previous and next are drawn for a film, because today’s page draws them for every kind. ±skip lands as a second transport row under the first. −10 s · ⏯ · +30 s, large and centred. Nothing a film cannot do is on screen.
Fits the screen No. In b-music the queue, Play here and Stop are below the fold at 412 × 915 — you scroll to stop something. Yes, in every state and in all three languages. The tallest stack — three rooms, one of them not reporting, in Faroese — fits by letting the square art shrink.
Where the place is named Twice already today: the sheet header Playing on Stue TV and the place line under the title. Twice on a plain playing frame, and the brief’s decision 2 is whether to trim it to one.
Subtitles Chips, which have to grow into the two-level sheet with flags, badges and S · M · L — at which point the chips are a door to the sheet and not a control. One footer button opening the component the local player already opens, with one extra line: Applies on {place}.
How much changes Less. It is the page that exists, with rows added. More. But the Cast remote already exists too, and A is mostly deleting the second one.
What a viewer notices Casting now looks like the thing in the sheet. Nothing, on the phone that casts. That is the point.

Not drawn, and said so rather than quietly skipped: loading (the remote is only ever opened on something that already exists — starting on the place is the real wait and it is drawn); empty (a remote with nothing playing is not a screen, it is a closed sheet); the TV; the Tizen surface; the receiver’s own screens; and landscape, which the shipped Cast spec already settles by centring the same layout.

The one mini bar

Today there are two: a cast bar and an “elsewhere” bar. These are one component rendered twice — art, title, the place line, a +N chip when something else is playing too, and ⏯. Nothing in it says how the place is reached, and the cast glyph is gone from it. That is what stops the two bars opening two remotes.

Web and Mac

  • Web, 1440. The same blocks in two columns — art on the left, the whole control stack on the right — against Soveværelse TV, with volume as an inline row under the transport. No Chromecast anywhere on this surface.
  • Web, 390. The phone layout, unchanged. Which is the point: it is not a third design.
  • Mac. The Playing page is the remote: the same blocks, two columns, with the queue inline for music and the capsule along the bottom. The capsule’s volume button opens a popover with the same sliders — drawn over Films, because a popover you open from the capsule is for when you are not on the Playing page; over the Playing page it put the same three sliders on screen twice.

Decisions — the lean is drawn

DecisionDrawn (the lean)Alternative
1 · Volume on a film playing on a TV One slider, as for speakers; the hardware keys drive it on the phone. In every film frame. Keep the Cast rule — no on-screen slider for a TV, hardware keys and a transient pill only. Not drawn as a frame: it is the same picture with the volume row removed, and the round would rather spend a frame on something a picture can settle. The argument for it is still live and it is in the brief: on a cast, volume belongs to the TV, and a slider invites a fight with the TV’s own remote.
2 · Device chip vs place line One control at the top that names the place and opens Move to…, with Stop in the footer for every kind. Keep both — a chip for switching or stopping and a line for moving. Not drawn: two controls naming the same place is the shape this round exists to remove.
3 · Someone else’s playback The same remote, transport absent, state line Olivar is watching, one card saying why. A smaller read-only card. The drawn frame shows what the lean costs: a lot of empty screen. That emptiness is honest — there is nothing to press — but it is the strongest argument the alternative has, and it is visible in a-someone-else.
4 · Stop’s word Stop everywhere, the shipped cast.stop_room string. Every footer in the round. Keep Stop casting on a cast — which would mean the cast frame and the elsewhere frame differ by one word, and the identity pair would stop being a pair.

Open, for Bárður

  1. Does the state line keep Playing on {place} when the pill above it already names the place? The round keeps it, because it is the shipped string and the shipped cast remote’s own shape. Trimming it to Playing is one line of i18n and it would remove the only repetition left in the lean.
  2. The three-up footer wraps in every language. Subtitles & audio goes to two lines in English and Undertekster og lyd to two in Danish, beside single-line siblings. The shipped Cast remote already does this, so it is not a regression — but if the footer is being rebuilt anyway, it is the moment to fix it.
  3. Noir on the phone is a darker background and nothing else. ravilo.css inverts the accent to amber for noir; mobile/ravilo-phone.css does not. The round reports the difference rather than inventing an amber phone — noir-a-cast-film is in the package as the evidence.
  4. Decision 1 is the one with real weight. The shipped reason for no slider on a cast — that volume belongs to the TV and a slider fights its own remote — is still true. The lean is drawn because the round was asked to draw it, not because the argument against it got weaker.
