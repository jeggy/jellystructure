Mac update — where a ready update waits, and what Settings says

The Mac app only, in both families: Desktop (600 dp and wider: sidebar, rail) and Mobile (narrower: the phone’s own screens, its bottom bar, its Profile page). Read top to bottom: what is fixed → the named directions → how they compare → every state the recommended direction holds → the four decisions → new strings. Nothing on this canvas is a TV, phone, web, cast or Tizen surface.

What is fixed — drawn as given, not reopened

  • Two families, never a fourth layout. Every direction is drawn in the desktop family (sidebar at 1000 px, rail at 720 px) and in the mobile family (a 400 px window, the phone’s screens unchanged under a 32 px strip).
  • Type. The system font in the chrome — the Settings window, the sidebar, the rail, the capsule. Sora + Space Grotesk in titles and tiles, and throughout the phone layout, which keeps its own type.
  • Light and dark. Every state is drawn in Aurora (dark); the ready block and the ready notice are drawn again in Daylight (light), so the prominent state is proven on both grounds. --ok, --warn, --bad carry success, the cannot-update state and failure.
  • What ships today is a line in Settings ▸ Account between Signed in as and Change password: Ravilo {version} for Mac is available · Download · a sentence about where to install it. Shown when the server is newer, never on a development build. It is drawn as it ships in Block B.
  • Sparkle 2 does the work. Its own windows do download progress (when it shows one), the administrator password and Install and Relaunch. None of them is drawn; the handover point is drawn and stops there.
  • Check daily, download in the background, ask once: Restart to update. Never interrupt playback. Nothing modal, nothing that takes focus, nothing that pauses. While something plays, no control that would stop it is on screen.
  • Never past the server. “A newer version exists but your server has not got it” is its own state with its own words.
  • An app that cannot update itself says so, in the shipped words, with Quit Ravilo as its only action. macOS’s own labels stay macOS’s; no internal names, no bundle talk, no version jargon.
How the tension is held. The ready state is the only state with a filled accent button and an accent edge, and it is the only state that reaches outside Settings — as a dot on the Settings entry and one row at the sidebar’s foot (on the phone: a dot on Profile and one row on the Profile page). Both sit where the eye goes between things, never over content, and neither moves, animates or takes focus. While something plays, the same row stays but its Restart becomes the words “After playback”: still visible, nothing to trip over.
