Radio beside the live channels, and a Radio row on Music Home

Three decisions, drawn: the radio mark that tells a station from a channel, the TV radio player that stands in for a picture there is none of, and where the Radio row goes on Music Home. Two more surfaces — a station playing in Music mode, and the remote's radio shape — are drawn as single answers, because the spec leaves them no second good one.

What is fixed

These are not the round's to choose. They come from the adopted spec and from what the live-TV surfaces already are.

  • One lineup. A station is numbered, ordered, zapped through and listed exactly like a channel. There is no second list anywhere — the live row, the guide, Now/Next, the zap order and the admin table all read the same six-entry array, in the same order.
  • One difference on the tile. A station's tile is a channel's tile plus the mark; with no guide it shows Live radio where a programme would be, and no progress bar.
  • The TV is a D-pad. Everything focusable shows focus, and a station tile focuses exactly as a channel tile does — the 4 px ring and the 1.05 scale the row already uses.
  • No timeshift. ⏯ stops the station; pressing it again re-tunes at the live edge. So there is no paused state to draw — there is a stopped one, and it says what the next press does.
  • The admin renders server-pushed state only. The mark is one class; the lineup table is the one that already exists.
  • No radio on Tizen, on the Cast receiver's screen, or in Ravilo Web's music mode. A speaker is headless. The web gets TV mode only, which is frame sets 1 and 2 unchanged.

Decision 1 — the radio mark

Three options, on the same tile, with a TV channel's tile beside them for reference. Every one of the three carries the accessible label Radio, and none relies on colour.

A — a corner glyph recommended

Gives: the one corner a channel tile leaves empty, filled by a shape rather than a word — so its cost does not grow with the language. The broadcast mark ((•)) in a dark chip, 46 px at 1920, which is a clear silhouette at three metres. It carries straight to the guide's channel cell, to ↑ Now/Next, to the zap banner, to the phone's tiles, to the Mac's cards and to the admin table, at five sizes, as one drawing.

Costs: a glyph has to be learnt once. It is the only option that does not also say the word on the tile — the word is in the guide, the player, the Radio row and the admin, so the glyph is never the only thing telling the viewer.

B — RADIO beside the channel number

Gives: a word, read before it is learnt, in the meta line where the number already is. Nothing is drawn over the art at all.

Costs: it takes width from the station's name, and that cost grows with the language — in Faroese the chip reads ÚTVARP and 5 ÚTVARP Harbour Radio 2 only just fits a 340 px tile. It has nowhere to go in the guide's channel cell, which has no number column of its own.

C — the word inside the LIVE badge

Gives: one overlay chip instead of two. The badge the tile already has reads LIVE · RADIO, and at three metres the wider badge is itself a difference you can see before you read it.

Costs: it makes a liveness chip carry a kind, and in Faroese it becomes BEINLEIÐIS · ÚTVARP — a red slab over more than half the tile's top edge, and the loudest thing on the row. It does not carry to the guide at all: a guide cell has no LIVE badge to extend, so C would need A's glyph there anyway.

Where the recommended mark goes next

Decision 2 — the TV radio player

A — the Now-playing layout recommended

Gives: a screen built for having no picture. One centred block — plate, name and number, LIVE, the programme with its times and live progress, then Next — and nothing pinned to an edge. Because it is one block, the burn-in step is one movement of everything on screen, which is the only version of that step that actually protects the bright text.

Costs: it is a second player layout to build and to keep in step with R177's. It says the station once, which is right, but it drops the bottom bar's habit of being there when you press a key.

B — today's chrome, with the logo where the picture would be

Gives: nothing new to build. The bottom bar, the number OSD, ↑ Now/Next and the zap banner are R177's, in the places a viewer of this TV already knows.

Costs: three, and the pictures show all three. The shipped field behind the picture is opacity: .85 and with no picture it turns the whole 1920×1080 into one saturated wash — it had to be dimmed to .22 here before the two directions could even be compared. The screen says the station's name twice and radio four times. And the bottom bar cannot take part in the burn-in step: it is pinned to the bottom edge, so the brightest, most text-dense thing on screen is the one thing that never moves.

How far it moves, and how often

The states the recommended player also holds

The audio layout is almost skin-independent. The live player is black with the channel's own colour on it, and the radio player keeps that, so noir and aurora differ only in the ink. That is consistent with R177 and it is reported rather than treated as a gap. The one element that does not move with the skin is the red LIVE mark, which is --lt-live at :root in ravilo-livetv.css and is already red in every skin today.

Decision 3 — where the Radio row goes, and what a tile is

Four frames, because the brief's option C held two questions at once — the position and the tile. They are drawn apart: A, B and D change the position with the same square tile; C changes the tile and keeps A's position.

OptionGivesCosts
A · second, square The row lines up with the album row above it, tile for tile, so radio reads as part of the library rather than beside it. Reachable without a scroll. 142 px of card for a two-line label; Beinleiðis útvarp is at the edge of what fits.
B · first Impossible to miss. Makes radio the first thing Music Home is about, above what the household actually owns.
C · second, wide Half the vertical cost of A, and room for a long programme title on one line. Two tiles per screen instead of three; a card shape that exists nowhere else in music mode.
D · last Nothing is pushed down. Radio is below every genre row — on a 412 px screen that is the end of a long scroll, and the phone is the only place a phone can reach radio at all.

A station playing in Music mode — one answer

The title is the programme, the station is the line under it, its logo is the cover, the seek bar's whole slot is replaced by a LIVE mark, and the transport is ⏯ alone. The four other transport buttons are absent, not greyed: a greyed Next on a live stream is a control that will never work.

The remote's radio shape — one answer

The place pill, the logo, the programme and station, LIVE, ⏯ alone, then R371's volume block: the group's own level, its rooms, and Add a speaker…. No shuffle, previous, next, repeat or skip — and no Up next, because the queue is gone while a station plays and a button that opens an empty screen is not a kindness.

Open questions

Q1 — Should the audio layout dim after a while? FR-8 says the screen stays on; it does not say at what brightness. A 28-minute orbit protects the edges, but a 312 px plate that moves 28 px still overlaps itself almost entirely at its centre. A step down in brightness after, say, 30 minutes with no key press would do more than the orbit does. It is beyond the brief, so it is asked rather than drawn.

Q2 — the admin's dim. .lineup tr.off fades a hidden row to 50 %, which is right for a channel somebody has deliberately hidden. It means the three new stations — the rows the operator has opened the page to find — are the faintest on it, in both themes. Worth a look; not this round's to change.

Q3 — the guide's time header is one channel column out of step with its own cells, in ravilo-livetv.css today. See the report; it is corrected inside this round's frames by one scoped rule and raised as a defect against that file.

Q4 — ravilo-i18n.js disagrees with the shipped i18n/*.json on at least On Now, Recently added, Playing on… and the Faroese Live TV. This round used the shipped file, as the house rule says. Reported, not fixed.
