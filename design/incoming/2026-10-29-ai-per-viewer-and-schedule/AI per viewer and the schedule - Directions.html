AI re-rank per viewer · the schedule · a Scan dialog that says what it does

Round ai-per-viewer-and-schedule, revision 1, 2026-10-29. The admin only — no Ravilo client, no TV, no remote. Four places, drawn at desktop and 412 px, in both themes: 38 pictures.

What is fixed before anything is drawn

  • The admin renders server-pushed state only. Nothing on these screens is derived, optimistic or worked out in the browser. Every sentence, count and price is the server's; the dialog selects the sentences that match its ticks, it does not compose them.
  • The surfaces already exist and are extended, not redrawn. Settings ▸ AI's job box, Settings ▸ Processing's feature row, the two-pane Scan dialog and Activity's AI card all ship today. Every picture in this round links the production wf.css, app.css and processing.css, so what is in them is the shipped system.
  • Shown, disabled, with its reason — never hidden. The owner's rule for a control that cannot do anything new. This round applies it to a dependent tick as well as to a switch.
  • Dark and light are both real. Every frame is drawn in both.
  • English only. The admin ships no translated strings; there is no Danish or Faroese draft in this round and none is owed.

The direction

There is one. This is an addendum to surfaces that are already decided, so the round's job is consistency rather than choice — with two exceptions, both below: the shape of the quiet-viewer control (two were drawn, one kept) and where the summary line sits (two positions tried).

1 · AI ▸ Re-rank job — one switch per Jellyfin user

A list inside the Re-rank job's own box, under its three status lines. Four columns: Jellyfin user, Ravilo device, Recommended row, Re-rank. Every row carries its default's reason — on by default as well as off — test account — because a reason drawn only on the off rows reads as those rows being special rather than as one of two defaults, which is the opposite of what "the default is never silent" asks for.

A long list is made usable by folding the test accounts behind their own count: 14 rows and one fold that says what is inside it, rather than a scrollbox inside a page that already scrolls, and rather than paging. A filter and three tabs sit above it; every count in them is the server's.

2 · Settings ▸ Processing — two pickers, two quiet-viewer choices

Each part takes the picker Find new & changed already has, with Monthly on added: a day from 1 to 28 and a time, with the reason for 28 beside the number rather than in a paragraph under the row. Under each picker, When nobody has watched anything new as a select with the chosen option's own sentence beneath it.

3 · The Scan dialog — what runs, what is skipped, and what AI it asks

Three additions to the right pane, all server-sent:

  • One line per part saying what this scan does to it. Ticked: Rebuilds 4 viewers' lists now — the schedule would next run Tue 20:00. Unticked, the same slot: Not in this scan — next by schedule 1 Nov 04:00, dimmed. The line never moves, so "unticked" is never a blank.
  • A dependent and ask AI tick under each part, unticked on every open and never remembered, carrying what it would cost and what is left of the month. Shown in all three cases — live, disabled because the part is not in this scan, disabled because the limit leaves too little — so the part never changes height as its own tick is pressed.
  • One summary band above Start scan: Runs: … · Skips: … · AI: ….

4 · Activity ▸ the AI card

The held-back sentence stays where it is. Remove all 37 waiting goes beside it, its count the server's, with one line naming what it does not touch. On each waiting row the chip held by this month's limit replaces the shipped waiting badge rather than sitting beside it: one slot, one state.

Three live defects the pictures found

None of them is this round's own work; all three are in code that ships today, and all three are fixed in the round's CSS so the engineer picks them up with everything else.

  1. A time field clips in a 12-hour locale. Every type="time" in Settings ▸ Processing is width: 112px, which fits 20:00 and cuts 08:00 PM and its own picker button in half.
  2. The schedule check overflows at 412. The server's description goes inside a .badge, and every badge is white-space: nowrap; the longest description runs off the right edge of the card, cut mid-word.
  3. Activity's job rows are unreadable at 412. .jobrow has no narrow tier at all, so the controls keep their columns and leave the text about 40 px — queued by the schedule became one word per line with the chip drawn over it.

  2. Remove all 37 waiting has no confirmation. The spec names one control and no confirmation; Empty queues, one card away, has one. Drawn as the spec says.

  4. The brief's summary line names five of the dialog's ten parts, while the left pane shows the other five ticked. Its shape and its three clauses are kept exactly; the two lists are extended to name what each picture actually ticks.
  5. The brief's count sentence and its own list disagree: "4 people plus 5 test accounts" against a list of 5 people and 4 test accounts. The named list is what is drawn.
