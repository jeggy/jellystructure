Collection artwork — three logos and three tile gradients

Two directions across three collections for Ravilo's Home rail, drawn on the real tile: 224 × 94 dp, radius 13, the brand gradient at its authored angle, the 8 % sheen, the 6 % watermark, the logo fitted inside padding_logo, and the focus state at scale 1.08 with a 3 dp ring and a 22 dp glow.

Settled on 2026-10-29: direction A, the series hub is Ét afsnit til, and the colours stay as drawn. The hub's logo is finished in direction A and its file is upload/et-afsnit-til.svg. Direction B is frozen where it stood — its hub tile still carries the mark and the empty name slot it was drawn with during the hold, and it gets nothing more. Where this document compares the two directions below, it is comparing them as they were put to the owner.

What is fixed before anything is drawn

  • The tile. 224 × 94 dp, corner 13 dp, fixed on TV, phone, web and desktop. Fill is brand_color as a CSS-accurate linear-gradient at its authored angle; over it a white 8 % corner-to-corner sheen, then the name's first 8 characters uppercased in Space Grotesk Bold 29 sp at white 6 %, inset 12 left and 8 bottom; then the logo, fitted.
  • Focus. Scale 1.08, a 3 dp ring in the theme's focus colour — Aurora #8E82FF, Daylight #5B4EE0 — and a 22 dp glow in the gradient's first stop at 55 % alpha. The ring is chrome and never the brand colour.
  • The upload file. viewBox="0 0 320 100", role="img", an aria-label carrying the exact name. No <text, no <style, no class=, no <filter, no <image, no <foreignObject, no <script, no href of any kind. Every letter is an outlined <path>.
  • Ink. A white wordmark, at 3 : 1 or better against every stop of its own gradient. A second colour may appear in the mark only; none of these six files uses one.
  • The neighbours. Five collections are already on the rail, in orange, red and three blues, and the two focus colours are violet. Their real logos and names appear nowhere — they are third-party trademarks and they identify the library — so each is drawn as a neutral white house shape, a badge and a bar.

The two directions

A — house badge (recommended)

A white mark, then a white wordmark: the shape every logo already on the rail has. Each mark varies the play glyph so it says what the collection is — the series hub a stack of episode frames, Sagaer three frames linked in a row, Tidsmaskinen a rewind ring around the play glyph.

B — wordmark-led

The lettering carries the logo, with one small solid glyph beside the word. It is a handsome family of its own and it is legible at every size drawn — and it breaks the rail's pattern, which is visible the moment the eight tiles are seen together.

One line or two: Tidsmaskinen

Two lines, in both directions. The split is Tids / maskinen, which is where the compound breaks. Both variants are kept in variants/ so the other can be uploaded without another round.

Drawn and dropped

The tittle of the first i as a clock face. It is the obvious wordmark-led device for a collection called the time machine, and it fails at the size it actually gets: the ring is 5.4 units in a 320-wide box, which is about 7.7 px on the tile, and it reads as a smudge. It also leaves the second i with an ordinary tittle, so the first one looks like a defect rather than a device. Chromium and Skia agree about it.

Direction B carries a solid rewind badge instead, which survives the same reduction — the test it passes is the one the tittle failed: the same silhouette class as Sagaer's link, which is legible at 13 px on the tile.

Decided in the drawing

  • Two lines for Tidsmaskinen, in both directions, split Tids / maskinen.
  • The marks stay white. A second colour is allowed in a mark and none of the three needs one: brass on brass and emerald on emerald both lose against their own gradient.
  • The rail's five existing tiles are drawn as one neutral placeholder, not five different ones. Five distinct placeholders would invite the question of which is which.
  • Direction B's glyph for the series hub sits where B's glyph sits — left of the word — rather than centred, so the tile shows the glyph at the size the finished lockup will give it.

Was open — all three decided on 2026-10-29

  • A or B. The owner picked A. The lean was A, on the cap-height evidence above as well as on continuity.
  • The three hues. Kept as drawn — emerald, berry, brass. Sagaer's berry is the one nearest a focus violet; it separates because the glow is magenta and the ring is blue-violet, and a wine such as #9c3268 remains the one-line change if that is ever wanted.
  • The series hub's name. Ét afsnit til, and with it its wordmark on two lines, upload/et-afsnit-til.svg, and the watermark ÉT AFSNI. Nothing is left open on this round.
