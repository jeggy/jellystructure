Phone downloads

A viewer downloads a film or an episode to the Android phone, sees what is on it, and gets the space back. Drawn 2026-11-09 · Ravilo Android phone, 412 × 915 dp portrait, only.

What is fixed before a direction exists

  • This surface and no other. The TV, Tizen, the Chromecast receiver, Ravilo Web and the desktops get no download control at all. No frame in this round draws one.
  • The bottom bar keeps its five slots — Home · Library · Search · Discover · Profile. Downloads is reached from Profile's Account card. There is no Downloads row on Home or in any feed.
  • No viewer string names Jellyfin, LDAP, Quick Connect, a plugin or a status code. Every string this round introduces is listed below and none of them does.
  • A poster tile badges picture quality only — 4K and HDR, owner 2026-09-28. The downloaded mark is therefore not a badge in that corner and does not become one.
  • 48 dp touch targets, and nothing below the phone's 13 px body floor.
  • Every number in every picture is computed from one array (pd-data.js). The storage bar's segments, the entry totals, the Delete-all sentence and the shortfall in the sheet are the same arithmetic, so a reviewer can add them up rather than trust them.

The directions

The question the round exists to answer

Only the download control on the film page was drawn as two competing directions, because it is the only place where two genuinely different shapes were on the table. Everything else is one direction, with the brief's two other open decisions drawn as single alternative frames beside it.

Recommendation · open decision 1

Direction B, against the brief's lean. The brief leans to icon-with-label beside Play, and in English with the word Download that is the nicer picture. It does not survive its own state machine. Three of FR-8's five states are words, and two of those words are long: Danish Mislykkedes — prøv igen and Faroese Miseydnaðist — royn aftur. In direction A the Danish failed state squeezes Play down to about 82 dp — the secondary control becomes wider than the primary one — and across the five states Play's width swings from about 217 dp down to about 82 dp, so the page's one primary action moves and resizes while a download runs. Direction B holds every state at a fixed geometry in all three languages. Compare a-film-states-da against b-film-states-da; the pictures make the argument on their own.

Recommendation · open decision 2

One segmented bar with an inline legend, as the brief leans. The alternative (alt-downloads-legend-list) is not wrong, it is just expensive: three list rows cost about 100 dp more than the inline legend and push the first download entry below the fold on a 915 dp screen. The bar is the summary; the list below it is the detail, and the page should reach the detail fast.

Recommendation · open decision 3

Delete watched in the overflow, as the brief leans — and with a bonus the alternative cannot have. In the overflow each destructive row has room to carry what it frees: Delete watched · 7.5 GB, Delete all · 26.9 GB. As three buttons across the top (alt-downloads-actions-top) there is no room for a number, and a destructive control that does not name its own number is exactly what the owner asked not to have.

The downloaded mark

A 20 × 20 dp rounded square, radius 7, inset 7 dp from the poster's bottom-left corner, white (#f3f4fb) with dark ink (#0a0c13) and an arrow-into-a-line glyph at 12 × 12, stroke 2.4. On a 16:9 episode thumb it is 18 × 18, radius 6, inset 5 dp, bottom-left.

top-right · .pb quality badge (4K / HDR) — shipped, untouched bottom-right · .mwk watched tick, 18 dp, #2dd49a — shipped, untouched bottom edge · Continue progress bar, 3 dp — shipped, untouched bottom-left · the downloaded mark, inset 7 dp — new, clears the bar by 4 dp

It is deliberately not a green circle with a check: that shape and that colour already mean watched on this surface, and an episode can be downloaded and watched at the same time. A second meaning on the same shape would make the commonest case the ambiguous one. An alternative in the quality badge's own dark glass is drawn beside it in b-mark-geometry; it is quieter, and it is not the recommendation because it then reads as the same kind of fact as 4K, which it is not.

The action names what pressing it downloads, not what the selected row says. For a season those differ: the rows show the season's total per quality (FR-5), and only the episodes not already on the phone are queued — so Original 18.6 GB and 720p about 5.2 GB in the rows, with Download · about 2.6 GB on the button and the sentence that explains it directly above.

What this round leaves open

The full list, with the reasoning, is in REPORT.md. In short: the brief's 37.8 GB cannot be reconciled with the brief's own per-entry sizes, and every total in these pictures is derived from the entries instead; a swipe that deletes 7.9 GB with no undo is the one gesture in the round that can lose real bytes by accident; and FR-9's film-then-series order puts a running download about 700 dp down its own page.
