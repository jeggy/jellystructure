Downloading one episode on the phone’s series page

The control on the episode rail and its states. Drawn 2026-11-10 for the Ravilo Android phone only, 412 × 915 dp portrait, against the shipped design/ravilo/mobile/ravilo-phone.css. No TV, desktop, web or cast frame exists in this round: no other surface has a download control at all (FR-1).

What is fixed before a line is drawn

  • The rail is what is shipped. The phone has no episode page. A season is a sideways rail of 320 dp cards (SeriesDetailScreen → EpisodeCard), a tap on a card plays, and a Watched toggle sits under each card. The 2026-11-09 round drew a vertical list; that is not this surface and nothing here reuses it.
  • Nothing new is drawn on the picture. Owner: “for non downloaded things it should show nothing on the poster, like now”. The 18 dp white mark stays exactly as it is, and the control lives beside or under the card, never on the thumbnail.
  • The tap on the card still plays. The control is its own target, at least 44 dp.
  • The Watched toggle stays and stays reachable.
  • Three languages, nothing smaller than today’s smallest phone text (12 px, the toggle’s own label). No string names Jellyfin or a status code (invariant 12).
  • A viewer who may not download sees none of it, and neither does a phone whose config has no downloads (FR-2) — no chip, no control, no mark.
  • The five control states and their mapping are already shipped (DownloadPhase.controlState()). This round did not invent them and does not change them: every wait is Queued, and a pause keeps its percentage.

The two directions

A · Under the card recommended

The under-card line becomes 320 × 44 dp: the Watched toggle at the start, the download control at the end, flush with the card’s right edge. The toggle keeps its shipped look and gains a 44 dp row to sit in. The card itself is untouched.

frames: a-rail · a-page · a-page-360 · a-page-tall, each in en / da / fo

B · In the card body alternative

The card’s text area becomes a row: the text column, a 1 px divider, then the same control, vertically centred inside the card. The under-card line keeps only the Watched toggle, centred, exactly as today. No vertical height is added to the rail at all.

frames: b-rail · b-page · b-page-360 · b-page-tall, each in en / da / fo

The control

58 × 44 dp, radius 12, 1 px border. No state carries a word, so the control is the same width in English, Danish and Faroese — the fixed-width problem that squeezed Play to 82 dp on the film page cannot arise here. 58 dp is the width at which “100 %” keeps real padding; 52 dp was drawn first and the 3× study showed the number touching both edges.

Why Downloaded is the only filled state. Two earlier attempts are in the round’s history and both failed in the picture: a green check collided with the Watched toggle’s green check, 240 dp away on the same line; a green outlined arrow collided with the Download control’s white outlined arrow, making “on the phone” and “not on the phone” the same shape in two tints. Filled against outlined survives both, and it is the shipped DownloadedMark’s own logic — the control is that mark at control size, so a viewer learns one symbol and reads it in two places on the same card. The --good green variant is drawn beside it at 3× in a-study-control; the white is louder and that is the only argument against it.

The status line

While a download is not at rest — queued, waiting for Wi-Fi, waiting for a connection, waiting for the server, paused or failed — the card’s air date line is replaced by the word for where it is. Never a new line, never on the picture, never any extra height. A resting card (nothing asked for, or fully downloaded) keeps its air date and is exactly today’s card.

Compare a-rail with a-rail-icononly, and a-rail-waits with a-rail-waits-icononly: without it, all three waits are one clock glyph and a failed episode is distinguishable only by a small amber triangle 180 dp below the picture. The brief’s lean — the reason lives on the Downloads page — is what the icon-only frames show, and it asks a viewer to go to another page to find out why nothing is happening on this one.

13 px, one line, ellipsised: --ink-soft for a wait, #f0c07a for a failure.

Why download.paused is needed. DownloadPhase.statusKey() returns null for a pause today, so nothing anywhere says the word — but FR-7’s acceptance 15 requires that a paused copy “still reads Paused five minutes later”. The key the rail needs is the one that acceptance already assumes exists.

Why not action.cancel on the running sheet. In a confirm that word means “do not do it”; on a running download it means “stop this and throw the 273 MB away”. One word cannot be both, which is why the Faroese row is Strika niðurtakið and not a second Steðga.

What the round did not draw

  • Skins — aurora only. Nothing here introduces a colour that is not either a shipped token or a colour the 2026-11-09 round already landed, so midnight, noir, graphite and daylight should follow; they are untested and that is a fact, not a claim.
  • The hero’s action row and the page below the rail — Play / My List and the About, cast and related rows. The action row was settled by the 2026-11-09 round and this round must not redraw it; the rows below the rail are unaffected by anything here. The page frames carry the hero, the synopsis, the Episodes header and the rail, and stop.
  • The hero at its shipped 340 dp — the frames use 230 dp so the rail is in the picture. On the device the rail starts below the first screen.
  • 10-foot focus — no TV surface in this round. The phone is touch; the rail’s D-pad focus order is unchanged because the control is a new stop at the end of each slot’s line.
  • The iPhone bezel and landscape — the surface is Android phone portrait (FR-1).
  • Loading and empty — the rail reads the phone’s own disk for its phases and the episodes come with the page; neither state exists on this surface.
