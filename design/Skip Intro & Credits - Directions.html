Skip Intro & Credits — design brief READ ME
What this is

A Netflix/Plex-level Skip Intro + Skip Credits / Next Episode experience for Ravilo, driven by real per-file segment timestamps that jellystructure detects and an admin can correct — grounded in credits-and-intro-detection-2026-07-13.

Where the numbers come from (4 sources, cheapest→surest)
  • chapter chapter-title pattern match (near-zero cost)
  • heuristic ffmpeg black-frame + silence (works on movies & single episodes)
  • fingerprint cross-episode audio match (Skip Intro; ≥2 eps)
  • manual admin-corrected — locked, never overwritten by a scan
Decisions baked in
  • Prompt-to-skip is the default — a pill you press. An Auto-skip mode is a per-user Ravilo setting (your ask).
  • Stinger-aware. We merge TMDB's duringcreditsstinger / aftercreditsstinger tags: when a title has a mid/post-credits scene, Skip Credits never blindly skips past it — it offers "Skip to scene" and auto-skip is suppressed.
  • Graceful fallback. Any title with no detected timestamp behaves exactly as today (Next-Up card at 34s from end). The feature can only ever improve, never regress.
  • Admin lives on the detail page — the single editing surface, matching Artwork / Tracks / write-through editing.
Pick per row. These are atomic options to mix & match — a pill style from A, a credits model from B, then I wire your picks into the live Ravilo TV.html + admin series detail.
