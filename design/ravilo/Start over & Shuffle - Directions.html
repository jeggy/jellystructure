Start over & Shuffle on a series

Two asks for the kids' rewatch loop: reset a series' progress once it has been watched through, and shuffle a series — without adding clutter. Drawn on the TV detail page (1920×1080, shown at half size) and the phone detail page, with the stand-in kids' show Lundin og vinir (3 seasons × 13 episodes × 11 min) on Olivar's profile.

Why a finished series needs this

What the TV does today with a finished series (frame on the right, built from ravilo-app.js): every episode is ticked, so seriesProgressFrom finds nothing in progress and nothing unwatched and falls back to the last episode — the page says ▶ Play · S03E13 and Up next · S03E13, and opens on Season 3 (R150-2 picks the first unfinished season, else the last). To go back to S01E01 a viewer has to untick 39 episodes one by one (Season Mark all was taken out of the TV in the 09-27 audit). And the ticks never clear on their own, so Up next stays stuck on S03E13 until they do.

Shared by all three directions

  • Finished means every episode in the library has been watched by this viewer. A new episode arriving ends it, and the button goes back to Play · S04E01.
  • A reset only touches this viewer. Jellyfin keeps watched state per user, so a sibling's ticks stay.
  • A finished series opens on Season 1, where Start over goes, not on the last season.
  • Shuffle is on every series with nine or more episodes, not only finished ones (Q7, owner).
  • Films don't change. They already have Play Again and Mark Watched.

Questions for the owner

Q2 is decided: B. The other answers below are what the build assumes under B. Leans are marked, so answer only the ones you disagree with.

Q1What does Start over do to the ticks?

(a) Every tick for this viewer is cleared once S01E01 starts, and Up next works normally from there. (b) The ticks stay. Up next follows the episode after the last one played (Jellyfin's Next Up has a rewatching switch, so check its behaviour), and resetting is a separate tidy-up (Direction B's shape).

a · clear them on startb · keep them, follow on (built, under B)
Q2Where do they live?

A: Play changes to Start over, plus a Shuffle circle. B: both go in the Episodes section. C: both go behind one ⋯.

AB (decided)C
Q3How is an accidental Start over undone?

Under B the reset plays nothing, so (a) is not possible. Built: two presses. The first press changes the chip to Press again · all 39 back to unwatched for 4 s, and the second press resets. (b) A confirmation, the same shape as Sign out's (R340). (c) An Undo toast: phone only, because a TV toast can't be focused.

two presses (built)b · confirmc · undo toast
Q4What does Shuffle pick from?

The whole series, or only the season the page is on. A kids' show is usually watched as one big pile.

whole series (lean)this season
Q5Does a shuffled episode leave a resume point?

(a) No. A shuffled episode is ticked when it finishes, but stopping halfway leaves nothing, so Resume on the page and in Continue watching still means "where you were, in order". (b) It counts like any other play.

a · tick only (lean)b · like any play
Q6When every episode has played once?

39 × 11 min is about seven hours. Stop with the normal end of the series, or reshuffle and keep going.

stop (lean)reshuffle
Q7Who gets Shuffle?

Every series with nine or more episodes (owner, 2026-10-01), only finished series, or only kids' profiles.

every series, 9+ episodes (decided)only finishedonly kids' profiles
