Newest first — a series where only the latest episodes matter

Adopted design · direction A · 2026-10-29 · set

This is the adopted design, not a set of options. The owner picked direction A on 2026-10-29 and went further than A on the end of an episode: nothing follows one at all. The requirement is specs/ravilo/requirements/a-newest-first-series-lists-and-plays-its-latest-episode.md and each frame below names the FRs it draws. What was declined — the viewer's own sort control, an Up next card, the episode code as a secondary line, the dashed Continue bar and the reference frame of today's page — went with the round and is in git history only.

Every frame links the shipped stylesheet for its surface — ravilo.css, ravilo-player.css, ravilo-phone.css, or wf.css + app.css + detail.css — so every token, button, pill, card, badge, ribbon, seg and focus ring in these pictures is the production one. The classes this design adds for the Ravilo surfaces are in newest-first.css and are reference; the admin's .eporder family is production and lives in design/app/detail.css. Every date, code, count, duration and percentage comes out of nf-data.js, which refuses to load if one of them disagrees with the spec.

The TV frames are the shipped 1920×1080 stage, drawn at 1920 and measured at 1920. The phone frames are the shipped Pixel 9 412×915 portrait. The programme, the viewer, the dates, the episode codes and the four viewer states are the spec's stand-ins exactly; the poster and every still is a plain invented gradient and never a broadcaster's picture.
