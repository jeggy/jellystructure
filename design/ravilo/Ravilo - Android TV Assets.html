06

Packaging wiring

AndroidManifest.xml

How the resources bind into the leanback launcher + splash. Maps to the custom CSS-variable tokens used in the mockups; on implementation these are Android resources.

<!-- res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml --> <adaptive-icon xmlns:android="…"> <background android:drawable="@mipmap/ic_launcher_background" /> <foreground android:drawable="@mipmap/ic_launcher_foreground" /> <monochrome android:drawable="@mipmap/ic_launcher_monochrome" /> </adaptive-icon> <!-- AndroidManifest.xml --> <application android:icon="@mipmap/ic_launcher" android:roundIcon="@mipmap/ic_launcher_round" android:banner="@drawable/banner" android:theme="@style/Theme.Ravilo.Splash"> <activity android:name=".MainActivity" android:banner="@drawable/banner"> <intent-filter> <action android:name="android.intent.action.MAIN" /> <category android:name="android.intent.category.LEANBACK_LAUNCHER" /> </intent-filter> </activity> </application> <!-- res/values/themes.xml (androidx core-splashscreen) --> <style name="Theme.Ravilo.Splash" parent="Theme.SplashScreen"> <item name="windowSplashScreenBackground">#000B25</item> <item name="windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon">@drawable/splash_logo</item> <item name="postSplashScreenTheme">@style/Theme.Ravilo</item> </style>
!
The build also declares leanback support so the APK installs on Google TV: <uses-feature android:name="android.software.leanback" android:required="false"/> and android.hardware.touchscreen required="false". Asset production is design-side (this pack); the manifest + Gradle packaging land in :ravilo-android.
Ravilo — Android TV Asset Pack (R62 recolor). Vector masters in assets/brand/ are the source of truth; all PNGs regenerate from them in the #AA5CC3 → #00A4DC gradient on the #000B25 brand field (“D · Lit mark”). Drop assets/android/** into the module’s res/ and assets/store/** into the Play Console listing. Synced to jellystructure@main (R01–R21 done). The Gradle/Manifest packaging itself is implemented in the repo — this pack supplies the artwork that build consumes.
