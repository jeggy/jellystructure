A turn is one round-trip: Claude thinks, runs whatever tools it needs, then comes back with an answer. It’s Claude’s own unit of work, not something Towo invented. The number here is how many a session has taken so far.

Idle is fine. A session that finished sits here keeping its context — open it, type, and it carries on rather than starting cold. The After quota reset switch is per session and starts off. Towo checks every 6 hours and only continues sessions you armed — never a session that stopped for any other reason.
