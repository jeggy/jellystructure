Live TV on Home

Live TV enabledManage channels ↗

Live TV is woven into this home — never a top-nav tab. These options appear because Live TV is enabled in Ravilo → Live TV; turn it off there and this section disappears (Live TV leaves Home entirely).

“On now” row
A live row of what's playing now, placed among the Home rows. Selecting a channel tunes it; an Open TV Guide action opens the full channels × time guide.
“On now” row position
Where the live row sits on the Home screen.
Live TV collection
A Collection tile in the rail that opens straight into the guide — for viewers who want Live TV alongside their other collections.
