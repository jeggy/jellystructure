Radarrshow this tab

Adds a Top 10 tab on this user's TV with trending charts from a third-party source. Each entry shows whether it's already in your library, currently being fetched, or requestable — a Request then grabs it through Radarr and Jellystructure organises it. Requires Radarr to be connected in Settings → Download tools.
