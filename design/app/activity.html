Background jobs run on one shared pool of workers across three named queues. A queue never has more than one worker drawing from it at a time — that single rule is what keeps two heavy media edits (an audio re-order is an ffmpeg remux sharing one temp path) off the same file, while still letting a remux and a background detection overlap. Scanning keeps its own separate workers.
